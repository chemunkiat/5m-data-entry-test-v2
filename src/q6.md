# Question 6 — AI Literacy: Prompt Engineering

**Format:** Short Answer

---

Read both prompts below carefully, then answer the questions that follow.

**Prompt A**

> I am a marketing manager at a retail company and we have just finished a three-month campaign. My team has collected customer feedback through an online survey and we now have about 500 responses stored in a spreadsheet. Each response includes the customer's age group, the product they purchased, their satisfaction rating from 1 to 5, and a short written comment. I need to present the findings to our CEO next Friday in a way that is easy to understand. Can you analyse this data for me, highlight which age groups and products have the lowest satisfaction scores, identify the most common complaints from the written comments, and summarise everything in a short paragraph I can use as an executive summary?

**Prompt B**

> **Role:** You are a data analyst helping a retail marketing team.  
> **Task:** Analyse customer survey data from a 3-month campaign.  
> **Data:** 500 responses containing age group, product purchased, satisfaction rating (1-5), and written comments.  
> **Steps:**
> 1. Identify age groups and products with the lowest satisfaction scores.
> 2. Extract the most common themes from the written comments.
> 3. Summarise findings in an executive summary paragraph.
>
> **Audience:** CEO presentation on Friday.  
> **Constraints:** Keep the summary concise and free of technical jargon.

---

## Task 1 — Analysis

**(a)** Which prompt do you think will get a better response from an AI? State your choice (A or B).

**Answer:**  
Prompt B will give a more consistent output, but added more contextual information may improve the choice of words and theme in output

---

**(b)** Give **two reasons** to support your choice.

**Reason 1:**  
Prompt B consists of a structured format that clearly defined the AI’s role, task, dataset, analysis steps and target audience. This reduces ambiguity and makes the instructions manageable for iterations and more consistent for the AI to follow.

**Reason 2:**  
Prompt B clearly defined the required output and communication constraints, including a concise executive summary without technical jargon. This makes the result more suitable for a CEO presentation.

---

**(c)** What is **one strength** of the prompt you did NOT choose?

**Answer:**  
I think Prompt A provides more context of the activities and business environment, which might be helpful for AI in choosing the wordings and choice of theme for the presentation slides.

## Task 2 — Rewrite

Rewrite either prompt by borrowing **one element** from the other to make it stronger. Then explain what you borrowed and why.

**Your rewritten prompt:**

> Rewritten prompt:
> **Role:** You are a data analyst supporting a retail marketing manager.
> **Context:** Our retail company has recently completed a three-month marketing campaign. We collected approximately 500 customer survey responses and need to evaluate customer satisfaction and identify opportunities for improvement before presenting the findings to our CEO next Friday.
> **Data:** A spreadsheet containing each customer’s age group, purchased product, satisfaction rating (1–5), and written feedback.
> **Task:** Analyse the survey data and identify customer satisfaction trends, areas of concern and common complaints.
> **Steps:**
1. Calculate average satisfaction scores by age group and product, highlighting the lowest-scoring segments.
2. Analyse written feedback to identify and rank the most frequently mentioned complaints.
3. Summarise the key findings and recommend areas requiring management attention.

> **Audience:** CEO and senior management.
> **Output requirements:**
* Present key findings clearly and concisely.
* Include supporting numerical evidence where available.
* Provide a short executive summary paragraph using business-friendly language without technical jargon.
* Base findings on the supplied data and avoid unsupported conclusions. If the spreadsheet is not provided, request it before performing the analysis.

**What you borrowed and why:**  
I borrowed the detailed business context from Prompt A and incorporated it into Prompt B’s structured format. This gives the AI a better understanding of why the analysis is needed while retaining clear instructions and output requirements, improving the relevance and usefulness of the results for management decision-making.
